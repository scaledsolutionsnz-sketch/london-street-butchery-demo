Privacy

This website does not have any forms and does not ask you for personal information. If you ring or email the shop, we only use your details to answer you, sort out your order or book your home kill.

What we keep

Your name, phone number and email if you give them to us, plus what you ordered. We keep this only as long as we need it for the order and our records, and we don't sell or share it.

Other services on this site

The map is provided by Google Maps and the videos by Facebook. They load only when the map comes into view or you press play, and they follow their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you. Ring the shop on 06 868 5928 or call in at 19 London Street, Kaiti.
